DBT FUNDAMENTALS PROJECT — Labor Market & Cost of Living

DATA SOURCES
- BLS LAUS — monthly local area unemployment statistics
- BLS OEWS — annual occupational employment and wage statistics
- Census ACS — annual American Community Survey

METRICS
- Median salary for DE-adjacent occupations, per region
- Occupational employment size for DE-adjacent occupations, per region
- Cost-of-living proxy (median household income, rent, home value), per region
- DE-adjacent employment and wage growth rate, per region (multi-pull, over time)
- Age and ethnic demographics, per region (secondary, "about the city" context)

QUESTIONS
- Which of my four target metros pays DE-adjacent roles best, adjusted for cost of living?
- Which metro's DE-adjacent labor market looks tightest (lower unemployment + strong
  occupational employment in relevant roles)?
- How has DE-relevant employment and pay changed over time in each metro, once I've
  got a few pulls' worth of data?
